Privacy Policy – Ledger & Treasury

Deutsche Version unten

Android app "Ledger & Treasury" / "Bilanz & Staatskasse" (package com.ledgertreasury.game) Last updated: 2 November 2026 (rev. 2)

1. Who is responsible

The developer named in the Impressum (legal notice). You can reach the developer at the e-mail address given there.

2. Summary

Ledger & Treasury is an offline economics game. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request the internet permission.

3. Data stored on your device

So that the game works, it stores the following on your device only:

your saved game (the state of your company or country),
your settings (language, difficulty, game length, realism level, whether you have seen the tutorial).

This data contains no personal information, and the developer has no access to it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in the Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Android backup

If you have turned on Android backup on your device, Android may include the game's saved data in your device backup in your Google account, so that your progress can be restored on a new device. This is a function of Android, controlled by you in your device settings, and Google's privacy policy applies. On Android 9 and newer these backups are end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock. The developer has no access to them.

4a. Android System WebView

The game runs inside Android System WebView, a part of Android provided by Google. The app turns off WebView's usage statistics. If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, WebView crash reports may still be sent to Google under Google's privacy policy; the developer does not receive them.

5. Links to other websites

The in-game "Model notes" and "Legal" pages contain links to research sources, this policy and the legal notice. They only open in your browser if you tap them. The privacy policies of those websites apply there.

6. Google Play

When you download the app, Google processes data under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer receives only anonymous, aggregated statistics from the Google Play Console (for example, the number of installs) and, if you have agreed to share them with developers, crash reports.

7. Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because the developer does not process any personal data about you, the developer holds no data about you that could be accessed, corrected or deleted. If you have questions, contact the developer at the address in the Impressum. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde (https://www.dsb.gv.at).

9. Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, this policy will be updated and the date above changed.

Damit das Spiel funktioniert, speichert es ausschließlich auf deinem Gerät:

deinen Spielstand (den Zustand deines Unternehmens oder Landes),

Der Entwickler erhält über die Google Play Console nur anonyme, zusammengefasste Statistiken (zum Beispiel die Zahl der Installationen) und, falls du der Weitergabe an Entwickler zugestimmt hast, Absturzberichte.

7. Kinder

Die App erhebt von niemandem personenbezogene Daten, auch nicht von Kindern.
